# Consumer case preparation: research and product decisions

Research date: 11 October 2026. This is a preparation aid, not advice for a particular case, an outcome prediction, a firm ranking or an endorsement. No named firm or advocate was recruited, interviewed or retained. Public practice descriptions do not reveal confidential client workflows.

## Client needs and advocate needs

Clients need a clear objective, costs, alternatives, candid weaknesses, timely updates, the next action and an actual recovery plan. Advocates need a complete factual file: correct parties, transaction purpose, agreements and payments, dated breaches, adverse evidence, source locations, prior proceedings, realistic instructions and supported relief.

The app adapts public practice principles into six functional roles: client operations, lead consumer advocate, evidence analysis, research/drafting, independent senior review, and settlement/execution. Real representation still requires an enrolled advocate's conflict check, written scope and fee arrangement, confidentiality arrangements and acceptance of the engagement. A local review task is not an engagement or communication to a firm.

## Real judgments: what changed the outcome

The library uses official Supreme Court PDFs, not news headlines as legal authority. It deliberately includes favourable relief, rejected claims and procedural dispositions. It is not a statistical sample. Every listed matter arose under the Consumer Protection Act, 1986, regardless of judgment date; current-law applicability and later treatment require separate review.

| Official judgment and pinpoint | Actual disposition and decisive issue | Preparation lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SGS India v. Dolphin International, 6 October 2021](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2009/23411/23411_2009_11_1501_30614_Judgement_06-Oct-2021.pdf), paras. 22–26, PDF pp. 12–14 | Award set aside and complaint dismissed: the comparative sampling and timing did not sufficiently prove inspection negligence | Prove the specific duty, failure and causal connection; an attachment count is not proof |
| [Lilavati Kirtilal Mehta Medical Trust v. Unique Shanti Developers, 14 November 2019](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2016/39297/39297_2016_14_1501_18237_Judgement_14-Nov-2019.pdf), paras. 7–9, PDF pp. 16–21 | Maintainability restored and matter remanded, not damages awarded; rent-free nurse accommodation lacked a direct profit nexus | Record the transaction's actual purpose and nexus, not just the purchaser's institutional identity |
| [National Insurance v. Harsolia Motors, 13 April 2023](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2005/5607/5607_2005_5_1501_43491_Judgement_13-Apr-2023.pdf), paras. 42–49, PDF pp. 31–34 | Insurers' appeals dismissed; insurance indemnifying business loss was not automatically a commercial-profit transaction; merits remained for adjudication | Separate consumer status from coverage, deficiency and amount |
| [Kandimalla Raghavaiah v. National Insurance, 10 July 2009](https://api.sci.gov.in/jonew/judis/35035.pdf), paras. 11–20, PDF pp. 7–14 | Limitation dismissal upheld; years-later correspondence did not restart time after the fire and prolonged inactivity | Identify accrual and contemporaneous claim dates early; prepare a supported condonation request when applicable |
| [DLF Homes Panchkula v. D.S. | [Wg. Cdr. Arifur Rahman Khan v. DLF Southern Homes, 24 August 2020](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2019/27240/27240_2019_33_1501_23551_Judgement_24-Aug-2020.pdf), paras. 55–57, PDF pp. 52–53 | Buyers' appeals partly allowed for additional delay compensation; other charges and claimant-specific positions differed | Preserve promises, possession/payment records and settlements; assess each claimant and issue separately |

Limitation and purchaser-specific distinctions are fact-sensitive. For example, [Hindustan Safety Glass](https://api.sci.gov.in/jonew/judis/44750.pdf), paras. 16–18, concerns an insurer's handling of a promptly lodged claim; it should not be confused with Kandimalla's prolonged inactivity. [Laureate Buildwell v. Charanjeet Singh, 22 July 2021](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2019/27595/27595_2019_33_1501_28766_Judgement_22-Jul-2021.pdf), para. 31, also cautions against treating later purchasers as automatically ineligible in every refund dispute. These qualification sources are research notes, not additional selectable cases in this release.

No subsequent outcome of a remanded complaint was verified here. A remand or maintainability ruling must never be counted as a merits win. No success percentages are computed from this library.

## Official filing and rule sources

- [Consumer Protection Act, 2019](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/CP%20Act%202019_1732700731.pdf): consumer definition, jurisdiction, procedure, relief, limitation and enforcement; sections 2, 34, 38, 39, 69 and 71.
- [Commission Jurisdiction Rules, 2021](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/232278_1732705181.pdf): ordinary complaint thresholds use **consideration actually paid**, not total damages requested. Rules 3–5: District up to ₹50 lakh; State above ₹50 lakh through ₹2 crore; National above ₹2 crore. Other routes require separate analysis.
- [Official e-Jagriti complaint filing guide](https://e-jagriti.gov.in/static/media/CC_Filing.34e0fc5dd03b1d864cab.pdf), March 2025: Step 4 records cause-of-action date, paid consideration, claim amount and cause location separately. Step 8 covers the relevant document uploads; commission selection, declaration, preview and later actions remain subject to the portal.
- [Consumer Protection (Mediation) Rules, 2020](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/Mediation%20Rules_1732703912.pdf), rule 4: certain matters are excluded. The app does not automatically declare a matter suitable for court mediation.
- [National Consumer Helpline](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/public/index.php/about): a pre-litigation option, not a guaranteed resolution and not an automatic limitation extension.
- [Department of Consumer Affairs rule register](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/consumer-protection-acts): check amendments, commencement dates and applicable versions before reliance. A newly listed rule is not automatically effective for every transaction.

An advocate must confirm applicable jurisdiction, limitation, local requirements, affidavits, attestation, proformas, authority documents, original annexures and pagination. The calendar anniversary in this app is only an urgency marker; accrual, exclusions, continuing cause and condonation are not adjudicated by software. Scrutiny acceptance does not establish the merits.

## Evidence and verification model

An upload becomes a user-reviewed evidence record only after an explicit review and a page/message/timestamp locator. A supported allegation requires a recorded duty, a specific breach and a reviewed linked source. This reflects file preparation, not a finding that a source is authentic, admissible or sufficient.

A selected judgment requires a passage and relevance/distinction explanation. Selection is labelled **user-attested**, with an advocate check still required. An official-host URL on an outcome note is labelled **review pending**; neither a URL nor a checkbox authenticates an order or confirms its finality.

Relief totals distinguish additional cash, alternatives and non-cash requests. Evidence-linked money is not expected recovery. Order compliance records use amounts actually directed and received, not model predictions.

## Implementation and production limits

The new strategy data is isolated under `cc-strategy-v1`; earlier intake and legacy review/outcome records are preserved. Exports and snapshots include the actual recorded file, assessment and selected authorities. Snapshots are deep copies and do not recursively embed previous snapshots.

This is still a browser-local prototype. Original documents are not persistently stored, client records are not encrypted by this code, and no lawyer is automatically assigned. Do not put real sensitive documents in the demo. Production needs authenticated case access, encrypted object storage, malware scanning, audited consent and retention, secure reviewer permissions, reviewed legal updates and a real advocate network.

Submission is assisted only. e-Jagriti credentials, OTPs and CAPTCHA are not collected, solved or bypassed; users complete authentication, payment and final submission on the official portal unless an authorised integration is independently available and approved.
